# Direct Lead Targeting Plan

Objective: build 50-100 direct leads in each of these four cohorts across London and Europe.

## Cohort targets

1. Company-let investors
2. Developers
3. Private landlords
4. Direct hotel buyers and decision makers
5. Direct hotel sellers

Recommended target: 60 leads per cohort, with a minimum of 50 and a stretch target of 100. That gives a total target of 240-500 prospect records.

## Target segmentation

### 1) Company-let investors

Goal: 50-100 direct contacts in London and major European cities.

Primary targets:
- Corporate accommodation investors
- Serviced apartment operators
- Short-stay property funds
- Family office real-estate arms
- Local property syndicates and HMO groups
- UK and EU corporate-let acquisition vehicles

Typical geographies:
- London
- Manchester
- Birmingham
- Edinburgh
- Paris
- Berlin
- Amsterdam
- Dublin
- Madrid
- Lisbon

Best source channels:
- Companies House / official company pages
- LinkedIn sales navigator
- Agent/management company directories
- Property Investment Network
- UK serviced apartment operator directories
- Real-estate crowdfunding and family-office databases

Record fields:
- Company name
- City / country
- Website
- Direct contact name
- Role
- Email or enquiry form URL
- Phone number if publicly listed
- Deal focus
- Source
- Status

## Recommended data capture structure

Each record should include:
- Segment
- Company name
- Country
- City
- Deal type
- Contact name
- Job title
- Decision-maker flag
- Email / web form
- Phone
- Website
- Source URL
- Lead score
- Notes
- Status (new / contacted / warm / rejected / meeting booked)

## Operational approach

1. Build a master pipeline of 250-500 records in a spreadsheet or CRM.
2. Filter to London and key European cities with active commercial property / hotel acquisition flow.
3. Prioritise decision-makers and direct ownership contacts over generic sales teams.
4. Score each lead on fit, size, urgency, and likely deal velocity.
5. Start with 60 per cohort, then broaden to 100 if outreach response is weak.

## Most important note

The goal is direct, qualified, company-level outreach to real acquisition and ownership decision-makers. Do not rely on generic lists or scraped personal data without verification. Use official company pages, investor pages, broker listings, and direct public contact routes as the primary source of truth.
