# PQ COMMAND Operating Model

Date: 2026-08-28

## Daily Execution

### Night / Background

- Discover
- Research
- Verify
- Deduplicate
- Score
- Prepare
- Constant sourcing bot keeps recurring background workers alive without a manual morning start.

### 08:30 Weekdays

- Outreach queue opens
- Approval / controlled send
- Replies monitored

### Daytime

- Hot reply
- Requirement / stock qualified
- Match
- Viewing
- Offer
- Deal

### Evening

- Results captured
- Source and campaign performance reviewed
- New shortage targets created
- Next discovery cycle prepared

## Agent Daily Interface

1. Open Command Centre.
2. Handle HOT replies first.
3. Review priority leads.
4. Approve or edit outbound queue.
5. Work qualified matches.
6. Book or complete viewings.
7. Progress deals.
8. Review AI activity only where exceptions exist.
9. End day with clean next actions.

## Commercial North Star

- Primary outcome: 5 to 10 completed company lets per week.
- Stretch outcome: 10 completed company lets per week.
- Measure the real funnel, not assumed ratios.
- Track multi-unit opportunities separately.
- Track pipeline and weighted pipeline from commercial deal values.

## Management Scoreboard Questions

- Supply: are we increasing usable stock?
- Demand: are we creating direct demand?
- Quality: is the AI finding real leads?
- Outreach: are messages starting conversations?
- Match: can we connect supply and demand?
- Viewing: are matches converting?
- Deal: is the machine producing revenue?
- Efficiency: is automation reducing agent hours per deal?
- Source: which sources deserve more investment?
- Gap: where should sourcing intensify?

## Operating Philosophy

- Be aggressive about discovery.
- Be conservative about facts.
- Be disciplined about outreach.
- Be fast about replies.
- Be ruthless about lead quality.
- Be obsessive about turning demand gaps into new supply acquisition.

## Non-Negotiables

- Treat PQ COMMAND as one relationship graph, not disconnected CRMs.
- Preserve provenance, evidence, confidence, and history.
- Surface relationship-expansion opportunities without inventing facts.
- No agents in the automated direct-owner stock acquisition queue.
- Directness is a hard eligibility gate.
- UNKNOWN never equals DIRECT.
- Weak ownership signals never justify inference.
- Every automated outreach recipient must pass directness, suppression, timing, frequency, and compliance gates.
